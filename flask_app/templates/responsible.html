Student guidelines

  • Follow course-specific rules for permitted AI use.
  • Disclose AI assistance when required.
  • Verify facts, calculations and citations.
  • Protect personal and confidential information.
  • Do not submit generated work as original when prohibited.

AI-resilient assessment

  • Supervised in-class problem solving
  • Oral explanation and viva
  • Project demonstrations and practical work
  • Personalized case analysis
  • Reflective journals and process evidence
  • Transparent rubrics and human review

AI detector scores alone are not proof of misconduct.
